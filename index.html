General Rules and Regulations

These rules apply to every event at VYBE2K26. Please read them before you register.

Eligibility & Identification

  1. All participants must carry a valid college ID card at all times during the fest.
  2. In group events, all team members must be from the same college.
  3. A college may register a maximum of three teams for the same event.

Registration & Payment

  1. Receipts for all payments, including online payments, will be issued only at the registration desk.
  2. Participants must keep their receipts safe. Duplicate receipts will not be issued under any circumstances.
  3. Registration fees are non-refundable.

Conduct

  1. Participants must strictly follow all rules and regulations and cooperate with the volunteers and organising committee.
  2. Participants are responsible for their own belongings. The organisers are not liable for any loss or damage.
  3. Any form of misconduct will lead to disqualification.
  4. Any damage to college property will invite strict action, and the cost of the damage will be recovered from the participant.
  5. Consumption or possession of alcohol, tobacco, drugs or any prohibited substance on campus is strictly forbidden and will lead to immediate disqualification.

Competitions

  1. Offensive, obscene or inappropriate content in any form (speech, music, costumes, visuals or performance) is strictly prohibited.
  2. Participants must clear away their sets and props immediately after their performance.
  3. Participants must report at the venue at least 30 minutes before their event begins. Late teams may be disqualified.
  4. The judges’ decision is final and binding, and must be respected.
  5. The organising committee reserves the right to modify the rules, schedule or venue if necessary. Any changes will be communicated in advance.

About VYBE2K26

VYBE2K26 is the annual college fest of KLE Society's Degree College, run by the Karnatak Lingayat Education Society, whose motto is Truth, Love, Service, Sacrifice.
